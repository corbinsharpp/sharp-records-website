6 November 2026

Sharp Records opens in Manchester

Sharp Records is a new electronic label based in Manchester, working with jungle, garage, breaks, ghetto tech and dubstep. The aim is simple: club records with the discipline of a good pressing and the bite of a pirate-radio flyer.

The first catalogue number is already in motion.

Signing

First signing: Bloom’s Village

Will Holton, recording as Bloom’s Village, is the first artist on Sharp Records. His debut EP is three tracks of experimental electronic music. Titles are still being written with the tracks themselves.
