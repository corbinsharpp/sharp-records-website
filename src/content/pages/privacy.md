## Newsletter

The newsletter box is separate from the enquiry form. We only add you if you type your email and tick the consent box. That consent is what PECR requires for this kind of email.

If the newsletter is switched on, the address is sent to Buttondown, which stores it and sends a confirmation email. You are not added properly until you confirm (double opt-in). You can unsubscribe from any newsletter email, or by writing to corbinsharpp@gmail.com.

[Create the Buttondown account and paste the API key before expecting the box to work. If you choose a different email service, replace the name Buttondown here.]

We do not buy email lists. The free Buttondown plan covers a small list; if you outgrow it, their pricing is a matter for you.

Lawful basis: consent. You can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of emails already sent.
